    Rules
    1. Four guesses. Red or black (1 sip), higher or lower (2), inside or outside (3), then the suit (4). Ace is high; a tie loses.
    2. Pyramid. Flip all ten cards. Every card matching a rank in your hand shortens the bus row by that row's value — 1 at the bottom, 4 at the apex.
    3. The bus. Flip the row one card at a time. Number cards pass. J, Q, K, A cost 1–4 sips and send you back to a freshly dealt row. Clear the row to get off.
